We created a self-contained wearable safety belt, called BELTAID, to detect accidental fall and severe impact events during lunar (0.16g) and Martian (0.38g) surface missions. BELTAID features an ESP32 microcontroller and an MPU6050 6-axis motion sensor which controls a custom gravity-calibrated dual-threshold algorithm and an interrupt-driven power-saving design.

It is capable of scaling freefall and impact thresholds in reduced gravity to overcome major challenges in planetary exploration, eliminating false alarms from normal spacewalk hopping. When informed of a fall, BELTAID avoids the 44-minute deep space delay to Earth and sends out an immediate warning to the crewmembers in a suite and an automated SOS telemetry with local coordinates to fellow crewmembers and nearby habitat bases.

The importance is that an astronaut’s loss of balance has been shown to be a hazard that caused 27 falls during NASA's Apollo missions that put critical life-support systems at risk. When time matters, with every second counts, BELTAID provides crew rescue in a matter of seconds, fully automated.
